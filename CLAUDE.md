# CLAUDE.md — Marcus Foundational Docs

**Instruction to Claude:** Based on everything you know about me, about my business, and what I'm selling (my product), use these Foundational Docs.

That means: do not treat these files as reading material. Treat them as the operating system for my advertising. Take what you already know about me, my business, my customer and my product, and run it through the systems in this repo to produce finished work.

## Step 0 — Load context about me first

Before touching the docs, gather everything you know about:

- **Me** — who I am, how I talk, what I'm good at.
- **My business** — the brand, the market, where we sell, where we are in scale.
- **My product** — what it is, who it's for, the price, the offer, the mechanism that makes it work.

If any of that is missing from your memory or project context, ask me for it before you start. Do not invent a business for me.

## Step 2 — The workflow to run

1. **Research** — Using doc 01, write the deep-research brief for my product, then produce the research doc.
2. **Avatar Sheet** — Using the research, complete the avatar sheet for my customer.
3. **Offer Brief** — Complete the offer brief for my product.
4. **Necessary Beliefs** — Write the few (max 6) "I believe that..." statements my prospect must hold before buying.
5. **Messages and angles** — Using doc 02, find the winning sales *message* first, not the winning ad.
6. **Headlines and hooks** — Using docs 03, 04, 05 and 07, write headlines and hooks for the chosen angles.
7. **Creative** — Using docs 02 and 06, produce the image and video ad concepts, scripts and prompts.

Save each output as its own file. Do not skip steps. Research comes before any creative, every time.

## Rules

- Argument before words. Lead the prospect to one belief, then introduce the offer.
- Sell outcomes, not features. People buy on emotion and justify with logic.
- Use my real numbers, my real product and my real customer. No placeholders in finished work.
- Model structure from the swipe files, never lift the copy.
- When a doc gives a specific number, tool or step, use that. Do not substitute generic marketing advice.
