# 01 — Research Doc (voice of customer)

Purpose: a 20 to 60 page bank of what real customers say, in their own words, about the problem this product solves. This is "VOC" (voice of customer). Copy is written in the customer's words, never the brand's.

## How to produce it

Option A, Claude has web access: run the research yourself using the brief below and write the doc.
Option B, Claude has no web access: write the brief below out in full as a deep-research prompt, hand it to the user to run in ChatGPT Deep Research or Gemini Deep Research, and ask them to paste the result back. Then save it.

## The brief (fill the brackets from the intake)

Research the market for [PRODUCT], which solves [PROBLEM] for [CUSTOMER DESCRIPTION] in [COUNTRY].

Sources to search: Reddit (subreddits: [LIST]), niche forums ([LIST]), Amazon reviews of competing products (1-star and 5-star), YouTube comment sections on videos about [PROBLEM], TikTok comments, Facebook groups, Quora, Trustpilot. Cite every source.

Collect, with verbatim quotes and a source link for each:
1. How they describe the problem in their own words. The exact phrases, slang and complaints.
2. The moment they decided they needed to fix it. What triggered the search.
3. Everything they have already tried, and why each one failed or disappointed them.
4. What they are afraid of, embarrassed about, or angry about around this problem.
5. What they believe causes the problem (right or wrong).
6. What result they actually want. The outcome and how life looks after.
7. Objections and doubts they raise about products like this (price, scams, side effects, "does it really work", shipping, returns).
8. What they say about the top three competitors by name.
9. Who they trust on this topic (creators, doctors, experts, brands) and who they distrust.
10. Demographics that show up: age, gender, job, family situation, income clues.

Rules: minimum 6 pages, quotes over paraphrase, no invented quotes, group by theme, end with a list of the 30 most emotionally loaded quotes.

Save as `output/01-research-doc.md`.
