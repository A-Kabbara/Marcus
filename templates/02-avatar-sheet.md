# 02 — Avatar Sheet

Purpose: take the research doc and relate every part of it to one person, the customer you are selling to. Fill every section from the research. Do not add assumptions the research does not support.

## 1. Snapshot
- Name (give the avatar a name):
- Age, gender, location:
- Job, income bracket, family situation:
- A day in their life, one paragraph:

## 2. Demographics
- Where they shop, what they already buy in this category:
- Devices and platforms they use, where they see ads:
- Price sensitivity and what they consider expensive:

## 3. Key challenges and pain points
List each pain in their words. For each: the pain, how it shows up day to day, what it costs them (money, time, pride, relationships).

## 4. Failed solutions
Everything they have tried. For each: what it was, why they tried it, why it failed, how they feel about that failure.

## 5. Goals and aspirations
- The practical result they want:
- The deeper result behind it (how they want to feel, who they want to be, who they want to impress):
- What "fixed" looks like, in their words:

## 6. Emotional drivers
- Primary emotion when searching (fear, frustration, shame, hope, anger):
- Fears about buying (scam, waste, side effects, looking stupid):
- Fears about not buying (problem gets worse, missed time):
- Identity: how they see themselves and how they do not want to be seen:

## 7. Beliefs and worldview
- What they believe causes the problem:
- What they believe about products like ours:
- Who they trust, who they distrust:

## 8. Objections, ranked
Every reason they would not buy, most common first, each with the answer the research suggests.

## 9. Direct quote bank
At least 30 verbatim quotes from the research, grouped under: pain, failed solutions, desired outcome, objections, emotion. These are raw material for hooks and headlines.

Save as `output/02-avatar-sheet.md`.
