# 04 — Necessary Beliefs

Purpose: marketing is belief change. The prospect arrives with beliefs about the problem, the market and the product. Every asset we make either installs an empowering belief or removes a limiting one. This doc lists the few beliefs they must hold before they buy.

Inputs: the research doc, the avatar sheet, the offer brief. Read all three first.

## Instructions
Write no more than 6 beliefs. Each begins "I believe that...". Each is a belief the prospect must hold at the moment of purchase, taken from the research, not assumed.

For each belief give:
- The belief: "I believe that ..."
- The limiting belief it replaces (what they believe now):
- Proof from the research that this belief matters (quote or finding):
- How we will install it (the argument, the proof, the demonstration, the story):

Order them in the sequence the prospect must adopt them. This order becomes the structure of every ad, advertorial and sales page. Lead them to belief one, then two, then the offer.

Save as `output/04-necessary-beliefs.md`.
