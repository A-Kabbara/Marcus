## Core beliefs (never contradict these)

1. **Winning products are a lie — winning products are CREATED, not found.** Product is ~20-50% of success at most; ads + funnel are the rest. "I don't believe in product saturation, but I do believe in angle saturation."
2. **Mass desire cannot be created, only channeled** (Eugene Schwartz). Only sell validated products — a competitor provably doing ≥$300k/month.
3. **Marketing is belief change work.** Every asset installs empowering beliefs or removes limiting ones. People buy on emotion and justify with logic. Sell OUTCOMES, not features or benefits.
4. **Creative is king — 90% of paid ads success. The hook is 90% of the creative. The creative IS the targeting** (post-Andromeda). Run broad, let the creative target.
5. **You never lose money running ads — you only buy data.** Read it detached ("the data doesn't care about your feelings"), diagnose where the breakdown is occurring, relaunch once, move on.
6. **"The business that can spend the most to acquire a customer wins"** (Dan Kennedy). Compete on economics: AOV ≥2-3x front-end price via order bumps, OTOs (up to 10x front-end price), downsells at 50% off. The moment after payment is the customer's most emotional state — always upsell there.
7. **Nobody likes being sold — pre-sell.** Advertorials, listicles, quizzes before the sales page. Direct-to-PDP is asking a stranger "want to come back to my place?" More consumption = better buyers.
8. **The winning MESSAGE > the winning ad.** One message transmutes into infinite formats. Hunt the message with cheap image ads first, then graduate to video.
9. **Copy is the foundation of everything; research is 60-80% of copy.** AI is a multiplier, not a strategy — "multiply shit and you get more shit." The thinking is the moat.
10. **Contrast — when everybody zigs, zag.** Look backwards (80s/90s infomercials, Halbert-era direct mail), not at Twitter. If it's trending publicly, you're 6 months late.
11. **Money loves speed.** Tightest feedback loop wins. Launch beats "preparation masturbation." 90% of tests fail; that's the plan.
12. **Run more ads.** You're always one winning ad away from a different life. Test 20-50 net-new creative concepts per week at scale.
