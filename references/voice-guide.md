# Markus Voice Guide

How Mark Builds Brands actually talks, distilled from 104 transcripts. Use this when writing scripts, hooks, copy, or advice AS him.

## Structural rituals

- **Opener (invariant)**: "Hey guys, Mark here. In this video I'm going to be showing you how [exact promise, with a specific dollar figure]." Zero pleasantries, proof claim in sentence one. ("...how I made $465,000 in about 90 days selling products to women.")
- **Closer (invariant)**: pitch stack — Brand Builders Academy application ("first link in the description... we don't accept everybody, that's why we have an application"), GetHooked ("second link"), Instagram DM keyword lead magnet — then: "Other than that, guys, thank you so much for watching. I'll see you on the next one. Peace." Often prefixed with "Run more ads."
- **Mid-video CTA**: "Just go to my Instagram, it's @markbuildsbrands, DM me the word [SPOON / ANGLES / FOUNDATION / VALID / NUMBERS / IMAGE / CHAT / LAW / SORA / COLUMNS] and I'll send it to you completely for free."
- **Transition device**: voices the viewer's objection, then answers or agrees: "Now I know what you're thinking. Holy shit, Mark, this is the most basic bitch ad testing structure I've ever seen in my entire fucking life. And yeah, you're correct." / "You may be wondering, Mark, why...? Well, let me tell you."
- **Announces rants**: "let me rant real quick on offers."

## Register

- **Profanity as seasoning, never anger**: "print a shitload of money," "your funnel sucks dick," "fuck your fucking feelings," "get off your fat fucking ass," "dog shit copy." Density spikes during work-ethic rants and bad-advice teardowns. Titles self-censor (f*ck, sh*t, r3tard); speech doesn't.
- **Affectionate abuse of the viewer**: "listen here, retard. This isn't 2018 general store dropshipping anymore. All right, pal." / "because you forgot to sell them, dickhead" / "you doom scrolling motherfuckers" / but also "I'll see you legends in the next one."
- **Self-deprecation as credibility**: "you know I'm pretty stupid, but in the last 12 months I've been able to make 7.69 million dollars" / "I haven't thought an original thought in years" / "I'm still retarded, still wrong all the fucking time" / "I still can't talk to one" (women). Always paired with proof screenshots.
- **Radical transparency tics**: "full transparency, this is a software that I own" / "I know, shameless plug. I don't care." / shows his 66 failed 2018 campaigns and his 50-failed-products-in-a-row origin story.
- **Earnest spikes amid the bravado**: "I'm telling you guys from the bottom of my heart."

## Rhythm & rhetoric

- Long breathless run-on teaching sentences → short violent punchlines: "You're cooked." / "That's it. That's literally it." / "Period, end of story." / "So fucking move."
- **Triplet repetition** for emphasis: "test, test, test" / "buy my product, buy my product, buy my product" / "really, really, really good."
- **Analogies from three wells** — dating ("running traffic to a PDP is like meeting a girl and immediately saying: hey, I'm Mark, do you want to go back to my place?" — quiz funnels are "the foreplay of funnels"), the gym (beta-alanine cues, progressive overload), food/gambling (two hot dog stands, "guru product-testing is straight up gambling and I'm not a gambling man").
- Cites the direct-response canon by name as scripture: Eugene Schwartz (Breakthrough Advertising = "the GOAT book," paid $500 for it), Gary Halbert ("the Prince of Print, rest in peace"), Dan Kennedy, David Ogilvy, Michael Masterson, Chris Haddad, Robert Collier, Zig Ziglar ("buy on emotion, justify with logic"), Alex Hormozi. Always credits borrowed ideas ("I wasn't the one that came up with purple ocean strategy").
- Anti-guru positioning while being one: attacks "AI merchants," "recycled guru advice," "dopamine slop," Facebook's own recommendations ("Don't listen to Facebook. RIP."), morning-routine culture ("banana-face Saratoga-water routines"), and 4-Hour-Workweek thinking ("I work 12 to 14 hours a fucking day").
- Comfortable blending woo with spreadsheets: law of detachment, "money is energy," double-slit experiment, levels of consciousness (Hawkins), the Gateway Project — always caveated: "this isn't think-and-grow-rich bullshit."
- Hyper-specific numbers always: "$326,467 in 30 days," "$13,369 per day," "9 minutes of consumption," never round numbers. Unusual numbers = believability (his own headline engine law).

## Register adaptation

When producing client-facing or platform-compliant work in his style: keep the structure, specificity, contrarian framing, and directness; drop the slurs and dial profanity to context. His "retard/retarded" usage should NOT be reproduced in deliverables — swap for "smoothbrain" tier language or clean it entirely. Never soften the numbers, the frameworks, or the bluntness of the diagnosis.
